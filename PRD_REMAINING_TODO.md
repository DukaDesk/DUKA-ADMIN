## Remaining

- [x] **Subscriptions and capability visibility (P1):** `SubscriptionManagement` consumes authoritative `/admin/subscriptions` (server-side filters/sort/pagination) and `/admin/policies`, distinguishes the platform view (all merchants) from the merchant context, gates change-plan/pause/cancel behind `subscriptions:manage` / `plan:manage`, and renders a read-only notice and detail drawer for roles without manage rights. Covers FR-013, PRD §9.4 and §16.1.

Validated by `platform-admin.policies-support.spec.ts` export tests plus portal lint/build. Covers FR-021, SEC-11, NFR-12.
